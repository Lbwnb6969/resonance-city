# 共鸣之城 · 完整成品城市生图输入（严谨版）

## 任务

Generate production concept references for the **finished target city** of the game Resonant City (共鸣之城), using the project design documents and the supplied current-game screenshots. The output must show the whole playable city at its intended large-city stage, not an isolated tutorial diorama and not a generic fantasy city.

The supplied screenshots have three roles:

1. `当前实际大城压力测试.png` is the current large-city scale and river-layout reference. It is a stress-test capture, so improve visual hierarchy and material clarity while keeping the same restrained scale and density logic.
2. `用户补充_早期河谷Demo.png` is the early prototype's spatial language: large pale terrain, a clear river, sparse white roads, small repeated homes and lots of empty land.
3. `当前Demo_古典小景.png` and `用户认可_古典小景参考.png` define the approved classical material direction: milk-white stone walls, muted terracotta roofs, pale gray-green ground and foliage, clean isometric geometry, soft low-contrast shadows.

Do not copy screenshots' UI text, labels, debug circles, or unfinished artifacts.

## Product identity

The player is a city planner and conductor. They sow sound seeds on continuous terrain; the city grows automatically according to harmony. The player chooses neighborhood charters, key public anchors, parks, cultural buildings, bridges, a few sound roles and tuning choices. Ordinary buildings grow by rules. The city and the music are two views of the same growth events.

The final city should read as a **small organized orchestra**, not as hundreds of unrelated objects. A few districts carry melody or rhythm; other buildings are supporting pads, pulses, courtyards and deliberate empty space. Never draw literal musical notes, waveform graphics or a loud fantasy aura over the city.

## Finished target scale

Show a finished large-city slice with:

- 36–48 initial planned neighborhoods visible as readable clusters or districts.
- Approximately 400–600 buildings, with a later-growth horizon implied but not requiring a metropolis skyline.
- Neighborhoods may split as they grow, but streets and landscape remain legible.
- Buildings are small at city scale; detail is grouped into coherent roof and wall masses.
- The city is continuous and organic, not a grid or a collection of square tiles.

## Spatial composition

Use a wide 3/4 isometric / axonometric camera looking over a long river valley. The river is a major empty shape that separates two banks and creates a visual and musical dialogue. Include a few simple bridges as connection points, curved pale roads, green corridors, parks, courtyards and open terrain. Water, sky and intentional open ground together occupy at least about 25% of the view.

The main river should run from a narrow upper valley toward a broader lower section. A few distant hills, forests or gentle terraces may frame the city, but they must remain quiet shapes. The terrain is the instrument: river creates two-sided call-and-response, mountain terraces create height layers, forest creates soft green absorption and plain ground gives clear space.

## City morphology

Buildings grow into connected fabric:

- Adjacent homes share party walls and roof edges; rows of 3–8 buildings read as one continuous street wall.
- A few courtyards are deliberately left open inside blocks; courtyards count as usable negative space.
- Leave visible gaps between districts so the whole city can breathe.
- Public anchors occupy continuous, non-grid sites: a modest classical civic hall, temple, amphitheater, music pavilion, park, cultural garden or bridge landing.
- Only a small number of landmarks are visual protagonists; each has clear surrounding breathing room.
- Roads are simple and pale, following terrain and neighborhoods; no full traffic simulation is needed in the picture.

## Two expressive urban routes in the same city

Show both of these as readable differences, without turning them into two unrelated color schemes:

### Deep Flow (深流 / low-bass route)

Along the river valley, place a sparse continuous low-density band of darker muted roofs and longer building rows following the water and contour lines. Keep open water and low-contrast mass visible. At dusk, this district has a sparse continuous line of warm windows reflected in the river.

### Weave (织机 / rhythm route)

Elsewhere, show a denser but still small-scale commercial street cluster: repeated roof rhythm, short connected street walls, two or three pocket plazas that interrupt the wall, and a few brighter windows. The plazas must keep dark quiet space; do not fill every gap with lights or buildings.

## Era and material direction

The primary finished reference should stay close to the approved classical-to-early-modern palette. It may show a gentle era transition: a preserved classical core with pale stone, arches, columns and terracotta roofs, and a newer edge with restrained plaster row houses, slightly larger windows and a few glass or metal accents. The transition is gradual and local, not a collage of five unrelated styles. Historic districts and landmarks keep their original silhouette.

## Color, light and motion implied by still images

- Global palette is disciplined: no more than 24 semantic color slots and no more than three hue families in one view.
- Cream stone, muted clay/terracotta, gray-green land and foliage, dusty blue water, small warm window lights.
- Low contrast, soft contact shadows, one sun from upper left, shadows cast toward lower right.
- Paper-like 2–3% surface grain is acceptable.
- Day image: terrain colors are clearest, water has restrained horizontal glints.
- Dusk/night image: deep blue-gray ambient light, longer soft shadows, sparse warm windows; the river, roads and district silhouettes remain readable.

## UI and camera rules for the concept images

The final city should be shown cleanly, with no permanent side panel, no grid, no debug overlay, no labels and no title text. At most, a very thin beat bar may be implied at the extreme edge, but omit it if it competes with the city. The image is a visual reference for the game renderer, not a marketing poster.

## Hard negative constraints

No photorealism, no cinematic skyscraper skyline, no modern megacity density, no high-rise towers, no cyberpunk neon, no saturated rainbow palette, no black outlines, no glossy AAA realism, no giant monuments, no fantasy castles, no literal music notes, no waveforms, no dense crowds, no UI text, no watermark, no generic SimCity grid, no tile board, no debug pressure-test labels.

## Required output set

1. **Finished river-valley city, daylight**: wide whole-city composition, 36–48 districts, 400–600 small buildings, river/bridges/parks/connected fabric.
2. **Finished city with Deep Flow and Weave contrast**: same restrained city language, explicit low-density river band and denser commercial rhythm district with pocket plazas.
3. **Finished city at dusk/night**: same structure and camera family, sparse warm windows and readable water/roads, not a neon city.
4. **District-level production reference**: a closer view inside the finished city showing connected street walls, courtyard, park/cultural anchor and a few distinct building facades; still clearly part of the same city and same art direction.

These are references for an execution AI: keep the geometry simple enough for a Godot 2.5D implementation, while showing the intended final organization and scale.
