Invent the melody, chord movement and small variations freely while preserving this acoustic-electronic balance. No vocals, humming, choir, bright hi-hats or dance-music drop.

Soft wind-like air may frame the opening and ending without masking the piano. Freely invent the theme and harmonic route. No vocals, choir, drum groove, sparkling bells or oversized orchestral climax.

Return to the small piano figure and let the low background recede. Keep detailed attacks in front and dark, deep reverberation behind. Freely invent the theme, transitions and harmonic direction. Keep high notes soft-edged, the low register supportive and the room spacious without blurring individual notes. No vocals, humming, choir, pop drums, bright music-box bells or theatrical orchestral hits.
