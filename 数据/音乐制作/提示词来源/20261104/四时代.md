## 一、 四个时代的音乐语言具体设计（作曲 · 编曲 · 声音制作）

为彻底打破现有候选库中“慢速毛毡钢琴＋尼龙吉他＋大提琴长音”的同质化新古典配方，四个时代从孤屋阶段起就通过**音程与调式、节拍与句法、发声触感（Articulation）、声场反射**四个维度拉开听觉身份：

| 维度                   | 1. 古典田园河湾<br>*(Classical Pastoral Riverbend)*          | 2. 文艺复兴连拱城<br>*(Renaissance Arcade City)*             | 3. 蒸汽钟表与花房<br>*(Steam Clockwork & Conservatory)*      | 4. 现代生态交响城<br>*(Modern Ecological Symphony City)*     |
| :--------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- | :----------------------------------------------------------- |
| **调式与和声语法**     | **开放音程与持续低音（Drone）**：五度/四度空弦共鸣、大二度擦弦；G Mixolydian、D Dorian 或五声性自然调式。靠低音持续音与平行开放音程呼吸，**不用浪漫派功能大小调四和弦循环**。 | **教会调式与透明二至三声部复调**：Dorian / Lydian / Phrygian 半终止；标志性的 **4-3 与 7-6 悬留音（Suspensions）**、声部交错模仿与自然皮卡第三度/空五度收束，**不用现代和弦铺底（Pad）**。 | **半音邻音、大小调交替与六/九度色彩**：带手作精密感的模块化小乐句，常用大六度、小六度与属九和弦的轻巧转折，乐句收口干净，**不用厚重管弦长音涂抹**。 | **四度/五度叠置和弦、Lydian 九度与慢速音色包络**：和声极度疏朗（常几小节才换一个和弦），靠模拟合成器滤波包络、磁带微饱和与泛音折叠制造内生运动，**不用 EDM 抬升或电影预告片式轰鸣**。 |
| **节拍、律动与句法**   | **松弛自由拍（Rubato）与不对称句法**：6/8、9/8 摇曳拍或松散 4/4；乐句不必凑齐 4+4 小节，允许 3 小节短句后接 2 小节空弦余韵与呼吸留白。 | **世俗古舞曲步态与交错切分（Hemiola）**：庄重二拍子（Pavane 2/2）或轻盈三拍子（Galliard 3/4 与 6/8 交替）；声部此起彼伏（你停我起），节拍端庄而有弹性。 | **音乐织体构成的机械咬合与停顿步态**：靠声部间的**断音交错（Hocket）**、3/4 弹性圆舞曲（微滞后的第二拍）或 5/4 错位重音体现精密感；常有**“走几步、停一拍留白、再重新啮合”**的疏密交替。 | **极简主义错位循环（Phase-Shifting Loops）**：如 5 拍子的温暖电子脉冲与 4 拍子的柔和拨奏缓慢错位相移，形成永不机械重复的呼吸周期；节奏边缘圆润。 |
| **发声方式与核心音色** | **指腹、木腔与气声**：特殊定弦原声木吉他（DADGAD 指腹拨弦）、中低音区木笛/竹笛/单簧管低音区（仅吹 2～3 音短叹息）、轻弓低音提琴。**默认禁用钢琴**。 | **羊肠弦拨奏与无揉弦古提琴**：鲁特琴（Lute）、西奥伯琴（Theorbo）、维奥尔琴合奏（Viola da Gamba，轻弓少揉弦）、柔音维吉那琴（Virginal，毛毡止音）。 | **短促簧片、拨弦与通透玻管**：<br>• **钟表街**：巴松管、低音单簧管、大提琴拨弦（Pizzicato）、手风琴（Concertina）短促呼吸、中低音玫瑰木马林巴；<br>• **花房**：竖琴泛音、中音区钢片琴、无马达颤音琴、木管分解和弦。 | **有机电子为主体、原声为可选伙伴**：温暖多复音模拟合成器（Prophet/Juno 质感）、Buchla 式低通门（LPG）圆润电子拨奏、深潜正弦 Sub-bass，配少量毛毡钢琴单音或大提琴自然泛音。 |
| **空间声学与制作特征** | **近景木质腔体＋开阔河滩空气**：极少混响染色，保留手指滑弦、琴体木板共鸣与真实微风气口，高音温润自然。 | **石砌连拱廊的清晰短反射（Stone Loggia）**：15～30ms 早期反射、约 0.9s 干净石墙衰减，颗粒分明，确保复调线条互不糊成一团。 | **木作工坊的干爽瞬态 vs. 玻璃花房的通透漫反射**：钟表曲瞬态清晰、声像分离；花房曲顶部高频空气感开阔（不加闷罐低通），但音头圆润不刺耳。 | **立体声宽幅交响尺度**：此处的“交响”指**频段与声场的纵深关系**——近景干声微脉冲、中景温暖合成器织体、远景开阔和声冠层，层次分明。 |
| **城市复杂度缩放规则** | • **简单城**：单把开放定弦木吉他稀疏指弹＋偶发空弦五度，零木管旋律。<br>• **丰富城**：加入木笛短句呼应、低音提琴弓弦支撑与双吉他流水交织。 | • **简单城**：单把鲁特琴或低音维奥尔琴的二声部稀疏慢行，大量休止。<br>• **丰富城**：三声部维奥尔与鲁特琴交错模仿、舞曲律动与悬留解决。 | • **简单城**：大提琴拨弦与巴松管/马林巴的稀疏错位点滴，走走停停。<br>• **丰富城**：手风琴、单簧管与木质打击乐组成室内小乐队轻圆舞曲或花房重奏。 | • **简单城**：单一温暖模拟合成器慢呼吸＋稀疏低通门电子水滴脉冲，无旋律线。<br>• **丰富城**：多声部错位音序循环、Sub-bass 律动与大提琴泛音/毛毡钢琴的空间交响。 |

## 二、 可直接复制的四时代文字生曲提示词库（共 24 首完整作品）

### 使用说明
1. **适配工具特性**：每条英文提示词均为从零生成一首**完整、自足器乐作品**的独立描述（含稀疏起拍、主体展开、中段减法留白与自然衰减收尾），不依赖上传旧曲、不要求模型单出分轨。
2. **解决 `core` 含主奏的工程约束**：每个时代的 **01、02 号提示词**专门针对**“孤屋/简单城默认背景”**设计，在作曲层面消除了持续前景领唱（仅以时代特征织体、和声呼吸与微动机成立），打包后即便 `core` 固定为 `1.0` 也不会在空城里抢耳；**03～06 号提示词**分别对应围合庭院、临水/曲路、手作/特色街区与高地/黄昏/替换备选，允许克制的短句对答。

### 时代一：古典田园河湾（Classical Pastoral Riverbend）

#### 1-01 · 孤屋与初垦基线（简单城默认 · 开放五度木吉他与空谷呼吸 · 无主奏）
> **作曲与制作要点**：用 DADGAD 开放定弦原声吉他与大提琴低音空弦五度建立田园身份，无钢琴、无持续木管旋律，松散 6/8 拍，每两小节留出自然空气尾音。
```text
Complete instrumental acoustic folk piece for a quiet pastoral riverbank, slow and unhurried at 68 BPM in a loose 6/8 meter. A single fingerpicked acoustic guitar in open DADGAD tuning plays sparse, resonant open fifths and fourths with audible warm wood-body resonance and gentle fingerpad articulation. A bowed double bass enters occasionally with soft, grounding drone notes. No piano, no continuous lead melody, and no percussion. The arrangement breathes with wide two-bar rests, thins out in the middle to bare open strings, and ends with a natural decaying chord.
```

#### 1-02 · 轻营造日常底景（简单城默认备选 · 双木吉他交错拨弦与微风留白 · 无主奏）
> **作曲与制作要点**：G Mixolydian 调式，尼龙弦吉他与钢弦木吉他一左一右做稀疏的问答式分解音型（非规整琶音），不写独奏主题，靠音色质朴感与开放二度音程耐听。
```text
Complete pastoral instrumental track in G Mixolydian around 74 BPM, loose 4/4 feel with natural rubato. Two intimate acoustic guitars—one warm nylon-string and one mellow fingerstyle steel-string—exchange sparse, interlocking open-interval motifs separated by generous moments of silence. Accompanied only by subtle bowed viola da gamba harmonic swells in the low-mids. No prominent solo melody, no piano, no drums. Close, dry-warm wooden room acoustics with natural air. The piece develops through gentle harmonic shifts, drops to a single guitar, and fades on an open fifth.
```

#### 1-03 · 围合农庄与暖院（庭院/围合场景 · 大提琴松香低音与低音木笛短叹）
> **作曲与制作要点**：强化中低频 `body` 的院落包裹感；大提琴双音（Double-stops）与温暖指弹吉他构成底座，木质中音长笛仅在句尾吹奏 2～3 个音的简短田园呼应，绝不连续炫技。
```text
Warm pastoral folk chamber piece in D Dorian at 72 BPM, gentle 6/8 meter. A solo cello playing warm, rosin-rich fifth double-stops and a fingerpicked gut-string guitar create an intimate, enclosing wooden courtyard atmosphere. Every few bars, a breathy wooden alto flute enters in its warm middle register with a brief, two-note pastoral sigh before resting for several measures. No piano, no bright high notes. Natural acoustic dynamics with a sparse middle section where the flute rests completely, concluding on a warm, unhurried cello and guitar cadence.
```

#### 1-04 · 临水河湾与曲径（水岸/内陆流动场景 · 9/8 摇曳指弹与芦笛微澜）
> **作曲与制作要点**：用 9/8 复合节拍（3+3+3）的流水拨弦构建自然律动，配民间竖琴（Folk Harp）低音点缀与单簧管 Chalumeau 低音区的柔和短句。
```text
Flowing acoustic pastoral piece in A Mixolydian at 78 BPM in a lilting 9/8 compound meter. A fingerpicked acoustic guitar and a small Celtic folk harp weave a gentle, rolling water-like arpeggio texture with asymmetrical five-bar phrasing and natural breathing gaps. A warm low-register clarinet occasionally answers with short, lyrical three-note folk fragments, leaving more space than notes. Grounded by soft plucked upright bass. Intimate open-air riverbend soundstage, no piano, no drums, tapering off gradually into quiet harp harmonics and natural decay.
```

#### 1-05 · 木作水车与乡间晒场（手作/细节场景 · 曼陀林木拨、手鼓轻擦与吉他对话）
> **作曲与制作要点**：突出质朴原声木质颗粒感；使用温润的中音曼陀林（Octave Mandolin）单音点缀、指腹轻拍木琴腔与木吉他做手作呼应，不靠高频金属打击乐。
```text
Rustic, unhurried acoustic folk miniature in C major with open fourths, 82 BPM in a relaxed 4/4 meter. A warm octave mandolin and a nylon-string guitar trade sparse, plucked wooden counter-melodies with plenty of air between phrases. Subtle, soft finger-taps on a wooden frame drum and plucked double bass provide a gentle handmade pulse without a heavy beat. A wooden recorder adds a fleeting, cheerful grace-note motif only in the middle section. Dry, natural timber acoustics, no piano, ending cleanly with a decaying plucked chord.
```

#### 1-06 · 远山草甸与暮色炊烟（高地/黄昏/独立备选 · 宽幅五度弦乐与木管余晖）
> **作曲与制作要点**：拉大音程空间跨度，用中提琴与大提琴的自然泛音和开放五度长音铺出山野纵深，配稀疏木吉他单音，适合山坡台地或黄昏收束。
```text
Spacious pastoral instrumental for twilight hills, 64 BPM with free, breathing tempo. Solo viola and cello sustain quiet, open-fifth harmonies and natural harmonics, creating a wide, airy horizon. A fingerpicked acoustic guitar places isolated, unhurried notes in the spaces between the bowed strings, joined briefly by a mellow English horn playing a single wistful four-note phrase in the midrange. No piano, no percussion, no dramatic crescendo. The texture gradually simplifies to bare bowed fifths and a final acoustic guitar note decaying into silence.
```

### 时代二：文艺复兴连拱城（Renaissance Arcade City）

#### 2-01 · 孤屋与石阶初建（简单城默认 · 独奏鲁特琴二声部稀疏慢行 · 无主奏）
> **作曲与制作要点**：一眼可辨的古乐身份；单把文艺复兴鲁特琴（Renaissance Lute）演奏稀疏的二声部对位与 4-3 悬留音，石廊短混响，大量留白，零持续旋律线。
```text
Sparse Renaissance early-music instrumental for a quiet stone courtyard, 66 BPM in a poised 2/2 meter. A solo Renaissance lute plucked gently with fingerpads plays transparent two-voice counterpoint in D Dorian, featuring delicate 4-3 harmonic suspensions and long, contemplative rests between short two-bar thoughts. Occasionally supported by a single deep plucked note from a bass theorbo. No continuous lead melody, no modern synth pads, no piano. Crisp, clear short acoustic reflections of a stone loggia, ending naturally on an open fifth cadence.
```

#### 2-02 · 轻营造古街底景（简单城默认备选 · 维奥尔琴与西奥伯琴交错呼吸 · 无主奏）
> **作曲与制作要点**：用古大提琴（Bass Viol）的无揉弦轻弓长音与西奥伯琴（Theorbo）稀疏拨弦交替呼吸，F Lydian 调式带古朴自然感，不设独奏声部。
```text
Restrained early-music ambient chamber piece in F Lydian at 70 BPM, gentle 3/4 meter. A theorbo and a bass viola da gamba share a sparse, breathing dialogue: the theorbo plucks slow, resonant dyads while the viol responds with soft, non-vibrato bowed intervals and subtle 7-6 suspensions. No prominent melody, no harpsichord clutter, and no modern chord progressions. Clear, intimate stone arcade acoustics with short natural decay. The music unfolds through quiet contrapuntal shifts, leaves generous silent gaps, and resolves on a warm Picardy third.
```

#### 2-03 · 连拱回廊与内院（庭院/围合场景 · 三声部维奥尔合奏与悬留温度）
> **作曲与制作要点**：维奥尔琴合奏（Consort of Viols：中音、次中音、低音）加鲁特琴，展示文艺复兴标志性的声部交错模仿（Imitation）与温暖的悬留和声解决。
```text
Intimate Renaissance consort piece in G Dorian at 68 BPM, unhurried 4/4 Pavane feel. A small trio of treble, tenor, and bass viols plays warm, non-vibrato polyphony with gentle staggered imitation and expressive 4-3 suspensions, underpinned by a softly plucked lute. Voices enter one by one and frequently drop out to leave transparent two-voice texture and breathing room. Recorded in a warm stone cloister with clear short reflections. No modern strings, no piano, no heavy climax, concluding with a poised, natural renaissance cadence.
```

#### 2-04 · 石桥水道与世俗舞步（水岸/曲路流动场景 · 6/8 与 3/4 交错的轻盈古舞曲）
> **作曲与制作要点**：世俗舞曲语汇（Galliard / Siciliana 风格），利用 6/8 与 3/4 的复合切分（Hemiola）产生优雅的流动步态，鲁特琴与文艺复兴木笛短句一问一答。
```text
Graceful secular Renaissance dance instrumental in A minor modal at 84 BPM, featuring a lilting 6/8 meter that occasionally shifts into 3/4 hemiola. A plucked Renaissance guitar and lute establish a light, flowing rhythmic gait with clear articulation and open spaces. A warm wooden tenor recorder joins intermittently for brief, conversational three-bar phrases in imitation with a bowed viol, then rests. Crisp stone arch acoustics, no heavy percussion, no baroque pomp. The texture thins before a gentle, decaying final chord.
```

#### 2-05 · 百工连拱与制琴坊（手作/细节场景 · 毛毡维吉那琴与鲁特琴精密对答）
> **作曲与制作要点**：选用音色温润、带毛毡止音的古拨弦键盘（Virginal / Lautenwerck 鲁特羽管键琴）与鲁特琴做精细的短句模仿（Hocket/Imitation），绝非尖锐的巴洛克大羽管键琴。
```text
Delicate Renaissance chamber piece in C Mixolydian at 76 BPM, light 2/2 duple meter. A soft-plucked lute-harpsichord (Lautenwerck) with warm gut-string tone and a Renaissance lute engage in quiet, intricate hocketed dialogue and short imitative motifs, supported by sparse plucked bass viol notes. Every phrase is followed by a clear pause that lets the short stone room reflection decay. No harsh metallic highs, no continuous busy scalar runs. The interplay gradually simplifies to solo lute and fades naturally.
```

#### 2-06 · 望楼日落与石砌广场（高地/广场/独立备选 · 庄重帕凡舞曲与石廊共鸣）
> **作曲与制作要点**：适合开阔石板广场或黄昏望楼；采用慢速帕凡（Pavane）步态，西奥伯琴低音与维奥尔琴、柔和暗音管（Dulcian/柔和双簧古管）形成开阔而克制的古乐共鸣。
```text
Poised Renaissance Pavane for a sunlit stone plaza, 62 BPM in a stately 2/2 meter. A deep theorbo, two violas da gamba, and a mellow renaissance bassoon (dulcian) weave a spacious, breathable modal tapestry in E Phrygian-inflected minor. The voices move in slow, dignified counterpoint with wide vertical spacing, frequent rests, and luminous suspensions that resolve into warm major triads. Clear stone courtyard acoustics without muddy reverb. The ensemble recedes in the final third, leaving the theorbo and bass viol to close in stillness.
```

### 时代三：蒸汽钟表与花房（Steam Clockwork & Conservatory）

#### 3-01 · 孤屋与发条初动（简单城默认 · 大提琴拨弦与巴松管稀疏错位咬合 · 无主奏）
> **作曲与制作要点**：**纯靠音乐组织体现机械感**，零滴答音效！用大提琴干爽拨弦（Pizzicato）与巴松管（Bassoon）短音做稀疏的错位交错（Hocket），走三小节、停一小节留白，无独奏旋律。
```text
Sparse, handcrafted chamber groove for a quiet clockwork workshop, 88 BPM in a precise yet unhurried 4/4 meter. Dry cello pizzicato and a warm solo bassoon play an interlocking, syncopated hocket pattern where notes fit together like wooden joinery, separated by sudden one-bar rests of pure silence. A rosewood marimba softly touches warm low-mid chords every few bars. No clock-ticking sound effects, no metallic gears, no continuous lead melody, and no bright glockenspiel. Intimate timber room acoustics, winding down cleanly into silence.
```

#### 3-02 · 玻璃花房的晨间呼吸（简单城默认备选 · 竖琴泛音与无马达颤音琴微光 · 无主奏）
> **作曲与制作要点**：对应花房分支的简单城底景；保留通透高频与阳光空气感（不加闷罐低通），用竖琴自然泛音、软槌颤音琴（关闭马达）与低音单簧管的稀疏和弦呼吸，无突出主旋律。
```text
Airy, translucent chamber ambient piece for a sunlit glass conservatory, 74 BPM in a gentle 3/4 meter. Soft concert harp harmonics, a motor-off vibraphone played with soft yarn mallets in the warm midrange, and a mellow bass clarinet sustain sparse, breathing major-ninth and sixth chords. Delicate, syncopated plucked acoustic bass notes provide a light, floating pulse with generous pauses between gestures. Bright, clear glass-and-timber room acoustics without harshness or shrill highs, no lead melody, fading out on a warm lingering harmonic.
```

#### 3-03 · 暖雾花房与藤蔓内院（庭院/围合场景 · 手风琴、单簧管与弦乐慢圆舞曲）

```text
Warm, intimate chamber waltz at 96 BPM in a relaxed, elastic 3/4 meter with a slightly hesitated second beat. Warm wooden parlor acoustics, concluding with a poised, quiet waltz cadence.
```

#### 3-04 · 精密街巷与5/4弹性步态（水岸/曲路流动场景 · 5/4 拍木管与马林巴精密律动）

```text
Nimble, precision-crafted chamber instrumental at 92 BPM in a graceful 5/4 meter (3+2 grouping). #### 3-05 · 钟表匠廊与簧片木作（手作/细节场景 · 巴松、圆号、中音钢片琴与断奏交错）
```

### 时代四：现代生态交响城（Modern Ecological Symphony City）

#### 4-01 · 孤屋与生态初脉（简单城默认 · 温暖模拟合成器慢呼吸与低通门电子点滴 · 无主奏）
> **作曲与制作要点**：**电子声直接承担主体**；用温暖的多复音模拟合成器（Prophet/Juno 质感）慢速和声场，配 Buchla 风格低通门（Low-Pass Gate）的圆润木质电子脉冲，零原声乐器套路，无主旋律。
```text
Minimalist organic electronic ambient piece at 72 BPM, designed for a serene ecological landscape. Warm polyphonic analog synthesizer pads breathe slowly through gentle filter modulation in C Lydian, creating a wide, living harmonic field. A soft Buchla-style low-pass-gate electronic pluck adds sparse, rounded, wood-like micro-pulses in an asymmetrical rhythm with long silent intervals. Grounded by a subtle, warm sine sub-bass. No lead melody, no EDM drums, no harsh sawtooth synths. Spacious, three-dimensional production that slowly fades on a warm analog chord.
```

#### 4-02 · 轻营造绿廊底景（简单城默认备选 · 5拍对4拍极简错位电子循环 · 无主奏）
> **作曲与制作要点**：经典极简主义错位循环（Phase-shifting loops）；一个 5 拍子的温暖模拟电子马林巴/合成器音序与一个 4 拍子的柔和电钢琴/Sub-bass 脉冲缓慢错位，不断衍生细微变化而不疲劳。
```text
Hypnotic minimalist electronic and electro-acoustic background piece at 80 BPM. A five-beat sequence of warm, rounded analog synth plucks gently phase-shifts against a four-beat soft Rhodes electric piano dyad and deep sub-bass pulse. The overlapping loops create continuously evolving harmonic patterns in G major ninths without a prominent solo melody or repetitive fatigue. No drum kit, no cinematic crescendos, no sharp digital highs. Clean, wide spatial mix with natural tape warmth, gradually dissolving one loop at a time into stillness.
```

#### 4-03 · 垂直花园与共生庭院（庭院/围合场景 · 模块化合成器温暖包裹与大提琴泛音）
> **作曲与制作要点**：原声作为电子的温暖伙伴；醇厚的模拟合成器低中频（Analog Warm Body）提供围合感，独奏大提琴与毛毡钢琴仅以极稀疏的单音/泛音回应电子脉冲的留白。
```text
Warm electro-acoustic ambient chamber piece at 70 BPM in D major ninths. A lush, enveloping modular synthesizer bed with slow tape-saturated timbre evolution forms a cozy, grounded harmonic core. Soft, muted felt-piano single notes and a solo cello playing warm mid-register phrases and natural harmonics converse sparsely with rounded electronic micro-percussion. Electronic and acoustic voices share equal footing without a dominant lead. Intimate yet spacious acoustics, breathing through quiet dropouts before a gentle decaying finish.
```

#### 4-04 · 生态水系与光能流线（水岸/曲路流动场景 · 多声部极简琶音脉冲与柔光合成器）
> **作曲与制作要点**：用清澈、圆润的模拟合成器点状音序（Pointillist Arpeggiator）与延迟反馈构建水波与光能的流动感，配中音长笛气声与深沉电子低音，律动轻盈现代。
```text
Flowing organic electronica and minimalist hybrid at 88 BPM in E Dorian with major-ninth shadings. Soft, rounded analog synthesizer arpeggios and muted electric guitar harmonics interlock in a gentle, rippling polyrhythm, supported by a warm, pulsing sub-bass and brushed electronic shakers. A breathy alto flute enters briefly with floating two-note intervals that merge into the synth texture. No EDM build-ups or four-on-the-floor kick drums. Expansive stereo depth with crystal-clear midrange, tapering softly into a final rippling synth echo.
```

#### 4-05 · 太阳能工坊与精密生态（手作/细节场景 · 颗粒感微电子节拍、颤音琴与合成器对答）
> **作曲与制作要点**：现代生态城的“手作/细节”体现为**精致的微电子声音设计（Micro-sound / Organic Folktronica）**——圆润的合成器气泡音、指弹电吉他泛音与颤音琴在精准的留白网格中对答。
```text
Intricate, tactile organic electronic piece at 84 BPM with a clean, spacious rhythmic grid. Delicate low-pass-gate synth plucks, soft acoustic vibraphone notes, and muted clean electric guitar harmonics trade nimble, syncopated micro-motifs across the stereo field. A warm analog bass synth anchors the harmony with sparse, rounded notes and frequent two-bar pauses. Crisp, high-resolution modern production with warm transients and zero harshness, avoiding any continuous lead melody and ending cleanly on a decaying vibraphone and synth chord.
```

#### 4-06 · 天际生态连廊与群落交响（高地/广场/独立备选 · 宽幅声部交响尺度与慢速演进）

```text
Expansive electro-acoustic symphonic ambient piece at 66 BPM, expressing scale through vertical register spacing rather than orchestral bombast. Deep, grounded sub-bass and warm polyphonic analog brass-synth swells support interleaving minimalist keyboard patterns and distant, airy string harmonics. Voices enter in slow, contrapuntal waves across a vast, breathable canopy, leaving wide open midrange space. No trailer drums, no harsh distortion, no overbearing soloists. After a luminous, wide middle section, the layers recede one by one into a tranquil analog horizon.
```

## 三、 音乐总监选曲与工程接入速查建议

1. **首批验证最小闭环（每个时代先挑 2 首）**：
   - 若要最快验证四个时代在游戏中的可辨识度，建议每个时代优先复制生成 **`01 号（孤屋/简单城基线，无主奏）`** 与 **`03 或 04 号（庭院/流动特色曲）`**（共 8 首）。
2. **分轨与入包策略对齐**：
   - 各时代的 **`01、02 号`** 生成后，因其本身就是无突出主奏的稀疏织体，即便作为 1 层完整曲或标准 4 层包（`core=1.0`）直接用于简单城，也完全符合《专题B》2.3 节“背景优先、克制入场”的要求。
   - **时代四（现代生态交响城）**的合成器音色在分离模型（Demucs/UVR）中通常会被归入 `other` 或 `bass` 槽位；工程端打包时若强拆 4 层出现相位空洞，应直接按《接入手册》规范做成 **2～3 层包** 或 **双生空间混音版**，切勿因追求凑齐 4 层而损伤温暖模拟合成器的相位完整性。
